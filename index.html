Up or Down

v0.1 · KPP Studio

How to Play

↑

Swipe UP to fly over things on the floor.

↓

Swipe DOWN to fly under things hanging from the ceiling.

↔

Swipe LEFT or RIGHT to dodge tall things that fill a lane.

✕

Pick wrong and you crash! Clear as many as you can.

↑

Swipe UP to jump over things on the ground.

↓

Swipe DOWN to duck under low branches and signs.

↔

Swipe LEFT or RIGHT to switch lanes around cars, boulders and trees.

✕

Hit one and you wipe out! Go as far as you can.
